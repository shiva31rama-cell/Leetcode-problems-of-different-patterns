## Rule for marking a pattern mastered

A pattern is not mastered just because one problem was accepted.

Mark it as mastered only when you can:

1. Explain the pattern in your own words.
2. Recognize it from a new problem.
3. Derive the approach without looking at the solution.
4. Implement it in the active language.
5. State time and space complexity.
6. Explain why the solution works.
7. Handle at least one variation.
